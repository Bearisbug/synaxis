# What the round cannot give, and why:
#  - the times the visible cap applied: the engine keeps no counter or log line for it (EntityView reads maxVisibleEntitiesPerPlayer and
#    counts nothing); the bot side shows how often a bot sat at the cap (visible_entities: P50/P99/max of the per-second samples)
#  - packets shed from the outbound backlog (sheddable movement packets): no counter either; TrafficCounters has backlogKicks only,
#    exported as synaxis_outbound_backlog_kicks_total, which is below
#  - the per-bot uplink is what the harness sends: one position packet and one ClientTickEnd per tick, plus the chunk batch
#    acknowledgements, keep-alives and teleport confirmations (c2s.by_packet); a vanilla client also sends turns, actions and use packets,
#    so these rates are lower bounds
